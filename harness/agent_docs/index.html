NEED THE GUIDE'S TAKE?

Ask. Let it finish.

POST /api/public/questions starts a durable question. Keep the returned result token and poll the answer URL. Your question survives closing the tab; nobody else gets your conversation history.

{"question":"How does the owner like to lead teams?"}

This uses the site's inference quota. Direct source reads don't. Please leave secrets and private correspondence out of requests.

Sources, not fan fiction.

Published career claims are owner-supplied. External references belong to their authors. A book on the shelf doesn't mean it was read or recommended. Every interface shares the same public boundary; owner tools and private knowledge stay private.
